## Context

Playerok is a marketplace where players buy and sell in-game currency, accounts, items, subscriptions and top-ups. It serves the Russian-speaking market, has more than 4 million users and over 250,000 listings across 1,200+ games and apps.

## Problem

One home page has to work for two very different visits. Some people come for a quick top-up and want to pay in seconds. Others browse thousands of offers from different sellers. Sellers, in turn, need a reason to list here rather than somewhere else.

## What I did

- **Home page.** I’m responsible for its structure: event banners on top, a row of popular games, a quick top-up block, category tabs and the listing grid, plus the sections for seasonal events.
- **Seller landing.** Work on the page that turns buyers into sellers: what you can sell, the games available and how much you earn after the commission.
- **Game and app pages.** Pages like PUBG Mobile UC combine Playerok’s own instant offers at the top with the full list of seller offers, filters and sorting below.
- **Consistency.** Audit the purchase flow and core sections with the PMs and the design lead to remove inconsistencies.
